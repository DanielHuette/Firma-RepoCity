# Competitors

Write into a run folder as `.claude/run-format.md` describes, lens `competitor`.

## What to collect, per competitor

- **Promise** The sentence on their landing page above the fold, quoted literally.
- **Proof** What they put next to it: numbers, logos, certificates, named customers.
- **Price** The figure, the unit and the conditions. "On request" is a finding.
- **Audience** Who the material addresses, read off the examples they choose.
- **Channel** Where they show up: search terms they rank for, networks they post on,
  trade press they appear in.
- **Gap** What they do not say. The absence is often the opening.

## Rules

Quote, do not paraphrase. A competitor's promise rewritten in your words is no longer
evidence; the whole point is to see the exact wording the market is being trained on.

Take everything from their own public material and archive the page into
`daten/quellen/` on the day you read it. Competitor pages change without notice, and a
finding about a page that has since changed is unprovable unless you kept it.

Price findings carry `volatility: price`, so the freshness scan brings them back after
ninety days.
