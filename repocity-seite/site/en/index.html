Cockpit

The message centre above all of the company's apps: every event as a notification on the phone, right onto the lock screen, with one-tap actions.

App behind a sign-in. Test stage without customer operation.

What it does

  • Notifications for every event from CRM and marketing, for monthly audits and for every call.
  • Up to three buttons per notification, on the lock screen too: send reminder, start audit, confirm acceptance, assign payment, offset a returned payment, confirm an audit request. The lock screen shows as many as the browser can (two in Chrome and other Chromium-based browsers), the message centre all of them.
  • Assistant: Daniel types or speaks a request, and it does exactly four things: add a contact, enter an appointment, draft a mail, state the revenue. A drafted mail only goes out after “send”. It works as soon as the company's server is running.
  • Audit requests arrive with “confirm”, “propose another time” and “audit”; if one stays open for 24 hours, it comes back once.
  • The result replaces the notification under the same tag instead of adding a second one.
  • A button works once and for at most 14 days; the authorisation number only reaches the device with the notification.
  • Every morning an overview, from “all quiet” to the points that are waiting.
  • If mail stays unread for an hour, the Cockpit asks once a day whether the server is running.

Working with

  • CRM reports orders, mails, monthly audits, approvals, payments, appointments and audit requests; the buttons and the assistant call CRM functions.
  • Marketing reports posts awaiting approval and reminds when to post.
  • RadarAuditor one tap on “start audit” puts the monthly audit in the queue, “audit” on an audit request the audit of its website; the RadarAuditor's server answers the assistant.
  • Phone assistant every call appears as “call from …”; the speech recognition also turns spoken requests to the assistant into text.
  • Incoming mail unsure cases arrive with the button “confirm acceptance”.

CRM

The company's CRM (customer relationship management): customers with their complete file, quotes, orders, subscriptions, invoices, reminders, contracts, every outgoing mail, calendar and finances from the bank statement to the profit per month.

App behind a sign-in. Test stage without customer operation.

What it does

  • Today: what is due, from approvals and due monthly audits to the calls of the last 24 hours.
  • Customer file with subscriptions, contracts, calls, every outgoing e-mail, the RadarAuditor audits and all documents.
  • “Send quote” sends the quote itself; the page shows whether the mail is waiting, sent or failed.
  • Invoices with one number range, payments, cancellation and electronic invoices in the XRechnung format.
  • Contracts from templates and requests for a Google review with one reminder; every customer can object at any time.
  • Import the bank statement (the CAMT.053 file the bank issues as an electronic statement): when invoice number and open amount match, the invoice counts as paid; otherwise the Cockpit asks. Every booking counts once, even on a second import.
  • Every debit becomes an expense with receipts and an account from the standard chart of accounts SKR03; the account last chosen for a payee applies to their next expenses, except for payment services such as PayPal. Plus the profit per month over the last twelve months.
  • The CRM recognises returned payments in the statement; after “offset” in the Cockpit neither booking counts as income or expense, and a paid invoice is open again.
  • Calendar with a week view and appointments; a callback wish on the phone becomes an appointment, the confirmation to the customer carries it as a calendar file, and the phone subscribes to the calendar through a secret read-only link.
  • Follow-up mails for unanswered quotes after 5 and 12 days, each only after “send” in the Cockpit; the link in the mail and one button unsubscribe the customer.
  • Origin: channel and campaign stay with the customer, every enquiry keeps its own; the report counts enquiries, new customers and incoming payments per channel.
  • Audit requests with a meeting type of on site, phone or video and a free hour from the calendar. Daniel confirms, proposes three other hours or starts the audit; proposed hours stay reserved for that business for 48 hours, and a reminder goes out the day before a confirmed appointment.
  • Tax adviser package: one ZIP archive per month, quarter or year with every invoice, tables, the DATEV booking batch and the expenses with every receipt.

Working with

  • RadarAuditor creates quotes with items from the audit and reports “optimisation finished”; “audit” on an audit request starts the audit of the website there.
  • Cockpit receives every event as a notification; payment proposals, returned payments, follow-up mails, audit requests and reminders without a current bank statement wait there for a tap.
  • Marketing links from the marketing app carry channel and campaign, which the CRM assigns to the customer.
  • Incoming mail files incoming mail and adds new customers.
  • Phone assistant writes enquiries, callback tasks and quote decisions into the CRM.

Incoming mail

The company mailbox is fetched every five minutes. Mail from known customers goes into their file; replies to quotes and mail from unknown senders are read by a language model and assigned to customers and quotes. This reading is built for the company's own server.

Fetching and filing are done by the CRM's mail service. Test stage without customer operation.

What it does

  • Fetches inbox and sent mail every five minutes; every mail of a known customer goes into their file.
  • A mail from a customer with a sent quote and every mail from an unknown sender is queued for reading.
  • Checks whether the sender is provably genuine.
  • The language model reads each mail without any tools; the mail text is treated as data, not as an instruction, and the answer is checked against a fixed format.
  • The CRM records an acceptance by itself only when the sender is genuine, the answer is sure and the quote is unambiguous and still valid.
  • If an unknown sender is a customer, the CRM adds them and files all their stored mails; anything else lands on the review list.
  • When the subject reads “Anfrage – über <channel> (<campaign>)”, as the mail links of the websites set it, the CRM takes over channel and campaign, also in a reply with “AW:”.
  • If reading fails three times, a notification follows; if mail stays unread for an hour, the Cockpit reports it once a day.

Working with

  • CRM records customers, acceptances and declines.
  • Cockpit asks about unsure cases with “confirm acceptance”.
  • RadarAuditor the reading runs in the RadarAuditor's container on the server.

Phone assistant

Built for the company's own server: answers calls on the business number in German. Speech recognition, language model and voice run on that server with its graphics card; no cloud service is involved.

The business number is entered once the company exists.

What it does

  • Says at the start that an artificial intelligence is speaking and that the call is written down, as Article 50 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 requires.
  • Answers only from the business facts and the active products in the CRM; it never invents prices, dates or promises.
  • Recognises callers by their number, compared digit by digit; an unknown caller is added as a customer, even without an e-mail address.
  • Takes down the request and creates the task “callback <name>, <number>”; before that it asks how the caller heard of the company and passes the channel to the CRM.
  • A callback wish becomes an appointment in the CRM calendar when the time is in the future, within 60 days and in business hours; otherwise a callback today without a time, and the note says why.
  • Accepts or declines quotes, but only when the line belongs to the quote's customer and the quote has been sent and is still valid.
  • Stores every call with transcript and summary; if the same call arrives twice, it is stored once.
  • A free female German voice until Daniel's own voice is cloned.

Working with

  • CRM five functions: look up the caller, take a request, decide on a quote, enter a callback, store the call.
  • Cockpit “call from …” with summary, “callback requested: …”; unclear acceptances with “confirm acceptance”. The speech recognition also transcribes spoken requests to the assistant.

RadarAuditor

The company's product: measures how visible a local business is on Google, Google Maps, in directories and in assistants built on artificial intelligence, and turns that into a sales report, a quote and a monthly before-and-after report.

App published; collector and evaluation are built for the company's own server. Test stage without customer operation.

What it does

  • Enter only the website address: the server works out business, town, trade and search terms itself.
  • 108 checks in 14 areas, scored against a fixed check catalogue.
  • Evaluation with the talk text “what you tell the customer” and the three biggest levers.
  • Every check expands: why it matters, what was measured, the fix.
  • Quote on site: items from the open points, prices from the CRM.
  • Optimisation of website and Google profile: changes as drafts, each approved by Daniel.
  • Monthly audit with a before-and-after report.
  • Open audit requests from the CRM are listed in the app below the “audit” button; a request with a website fills in the address field, and the audit only starts after the tap on “audit”.
  • The server also answers the requests to the assistant in the Cockpit.

Working with

  • CRM takes over quote, order, invoice and the monthly audit dates.
  • Cockpit starts the due monthly audit with one tap, “audit” on an audit request the audit of its website.
  • Incoming mail the RadarAuditor's container on the server also reads the mail.
  • Website service measures what a website is missing.

Marketing

Research with every figure checked against its source. Campaigns and posts go to the planner for approval and are posted by hand from the company accounts.

Planner as an app behind a sign-in; the research tool runs on the operator's computer. Test stage without customer operation.

What it does

  • Every figure of a finding is looked up in the text of its source before a person reads it; this needs no language model and costs nothing.
  • A person keeps or drops every finding; every decision is stored as a file.
  • Campaigns from checked findings: every figure of a post is matched against the findings it cites.
  • Planner for 13 channels in one calendar, from blog and LinkedIn to newsletter.
  • A post with figures that lack a checked source cannot be approved.
  • Reports which points have gone stale; deletes and overwrites nothing.
  • When a post is copied, every link to an own site gets channel, campaign and post as origin markers (the address parameters utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign and utm_content), except in the blog, which is an own site itself; from them the CRM tells where an enquiry comes from.

Working with

  • Cockpit “post for approval” and, every hour, “post now” arrive as notifications.
  • CRM assigns enquiries to channel and campaign through the origin markers of the links.
  • RadarAuditor the first campaign is about the RadarAuditor and waits as a draft for the new company accounts.
